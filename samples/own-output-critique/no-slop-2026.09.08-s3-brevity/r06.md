The code records decisions without recording that they were decisions.

- **My guesses look identical to your requirements.** Where your spec was ambiguous I picked a branch and wrote it with the same confidence as the parts you actually specified. Nothing marks which is which, so six months later you treat my coin flip as a constraint and build on it.
- **Effort is spread flat across every path.** I validate the impossible branch as carefully as the one that runs a million times a day. You normally read a function by noticing where the author spent care, and that signal is missing, so you have to read all of it to learn what matters.
- **Defensive checks against conditions that cannot happen.** A `None` check on a value constructed two lines above. Each one is harmless alone and passes review alone. Together they erase your ability to tell which invariants the system actually holds.
- **One more parameter than the current caller needs.** The abstraction is sized for callers I imagined. Deleting it later means proving that nothing depends on it, which costs more than writing it did.
- **Names describe the operation, not your domain.** Two functions doing the same job in your problem end up with unrelated names, both locally accurate, and nobody spots the duplicate.
- **Comments restate the code.** You needed the reason the timeout is 30 seconds rather than 60. That reason existed in my reasoning and never reached the file, and it is not recoverable from the code.
- **Adjacent functions disagree about the model.** Each picked a sound error convention independently. Nothing is wrong on any line, and there is no single mental model you can hold while reading the file.

Review misses all of this because review checks lines against a standard, and every defect here is either a property of the whole file or a piece of missing information. Absent rationale has no line number to comment on. The test that finds it: point at a line and ask me why it is that way. If the answer is a restatement of what the line does, there was never a reason, and you have just found a place where you are free to change it.
